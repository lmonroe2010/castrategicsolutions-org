A row of big-number statistics on the deep navy surface, under a 3px steel rule, each figure with a short plain-language caption.

**Markup:** `div.ssi-callout` containing `div.ssi-callout__row` with up to four `div.ssi-callout__stat`, each holding `ssi-callout__figure` and `ssi-callout__caption`. Wrap a unit or symbol (+, /, $) in `span.ssi-callout__unit` to tint it `steel-400`. The consumer provides each figure and a 10–15 word caption, with its source year where it has one.

**When to use:** anchoring a marketing section with the need SSI addresses ("1,952 people experiencing homelessness in Sonoma County"). Numbers are the brand — lead with a stat when in doubt.

**Do:** use lining tabular numerals in the display face. Captions are `navy-200` on `navy-800` (8.2:1).

**Don't:** use a stat without context, or set captions in cream at full strength. `steel-400` units are large-text only (4.1:1).
