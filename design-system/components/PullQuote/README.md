A pull-quote in italic serif behind a 3px steel rule, with an attribution line, for conviction statements in the founder's or a partner's voice.

**Markup:** `div.ssi-quote` containing a `blockquote` (wrap one phrase in `<em>` to set it upright in `steel-500`) and `div.ssi-quote__attr` with `span.ssi-quote__who`, `span.ssi-quote__dot` and `span.ssi-quote__what`. The consumer provides the quote, the person and their role.

**When to use:** to carry a conviction statement mid-page, on `cream-100` or `cream-50`.

**Do:** attribute external quotes always; founder quotes may stand unattributed when the tone is unmistakable.

**Don't:** use more than one pull-quote per section.
